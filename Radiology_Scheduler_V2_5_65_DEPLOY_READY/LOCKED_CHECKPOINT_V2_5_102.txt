V2.5.102 LOCKED CHECKPOINT — PERSISTENT PREFERENCES + CLEANER UI

This release is cumulative on V2.5.101 and preserves the V2.5.96 monthly-baseline/no-catch-up constitution and all V2.5.97–V2.5.101 functionality unless explicitly changed below.

PERSISTENCE MODEL
1. SETTINGS are resident-level persistent work-style/account settings. They automatically apply to every future schedule that is not yet frozen until the resident changes them.
2. MONTH-SPECIFIC PREFERENCES are stored for one target year/month only. They do not copy into the next month.
3. LONG-TERM RECURRING PREFERENCES are resident/weekday rules with no month key. They automatically expand into future months until changed or disabled.
4. Month-specific SOFT requests may override an opposite recurring SOFT signal for that month only. Recurring RESIDENT HARD remains hard.
5. Already-published SYSTEM baselines are never retroactively rewritten by a later Settings or recurring-preference change.

WORK-STYLE
6. Preferred workday length remains persistent.
7. Weekday/weekend direction is restored as persistent SOFT work style.
8. Weekend direction can select the upper/lower layer only inside the already locked Saturday/Sunday SYSTEM water-fill corridor. It cannot widen structural fairness.
9. Strength -2/+2 is stronger than -1/+1 but remains below HARD/structural constraints.

UI
10. Deadline information is shown in Preferences, not as a frame across every app tab.
11. Long-term recurring preferences are edited in Preferences, not Settings.
12. Settings clearly states that work-style choices persist into future months.
13. Preferences clearly distinguishes one-month requests from persistent recurring rules.
14. The user-facing V3.0 badge/build line is removed.
15. Lithuanian app title is "Rezidentų grafiko sistema".
16. Reminder-start wording explains personal countdown email timing and gives a concrete example.

DATABASE
17. No V2.5.102 Supabase migration is required.
